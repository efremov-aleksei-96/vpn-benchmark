## Mandatory failure-driven audit (all future CI failures)

For every failed or inconclusive CI run, first read the complete available logs at the exact tested SHA and classify the failure: product defect, test defect, preparation/environment failure, or insufficient evidence. Keep the original failure and evidence; do not treat an unavailable check as PASS. Review the relevant runtime, recovery, ownership, firewall, fixture, probe, cleanup and workflow paths and their dependencies. Fix confirmed important defects only, on the isolated fast-test branch. When the cause is not established, add narrowly scoped, redacted diagnostics rather than speculative runtime changes. Repeat the review after each important finding and its fix; a clean review means no new important findings in the reviewed scope, not proof of zero unknown bugs. Run regression tests and verify protected source hashes; publish only after the fast gate passes for the candidate SHA. Then rerun required integration and reboot acceptance; any new failure re-enters this loop. Do not weaken assertions or protection to obtain green CI. Do not use production, change sealed v1.5 sources, or expand scope without a concrete finding. Record PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED and evidence by SHA.
